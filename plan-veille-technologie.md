# Project plan

## Veille Technologie

Track technology developments and explain them clearly.

<!-- _class: light -->

## Project goal

Help readers spot important developments, understand their impact, and find reliable sources.

**Expected outcome:** a clear, current, verifiable technology watch.

---

<!-- _class: light -->

## Problems to solve

- News is spread across many sources.
- Announcements and confirmed facts can get mixed together.
- Dates, links, and implications can be hard to find.
- Overly technical summaries are difficult to scan.

---

<!-- _class: light -->

## Work to be done

1. Choose a topic and time period.
2. Verify claims using reliable sources.
3. Summarize what changed and why it matters.
4. Include the date, topic, and source links.
5. List what to watch without presenting predictions as facts.

---

## Technologies and scope

Use the stack already planned for the project:

- Laravel and PHP for the server;
- MySQL for data;
- Blade and Tailwind CSS for pages;
- Preline UI for interface components.

Add JavaScript, Alpine.js, or Kotlin only when a feature needs it.

---

## A technology-watch update

Each update includes:

- a title and topic;
- event and publication dates, when known;
- a short summary;
- why the development matters;
- sources and items to watch.

---

## Validation

An update is ready when:

- its claims can be checked against the cited sources;
- dates and facts are clearly distinguished;
- the summary explains what changed and who is affected;
- links work and the text is easy to read.

---

## Example: an MCP update

**Topic:** Model Context Protocol (MCP)  
**Question:** How do AI assistants connect to tools, and what has changed in the standard?  
**Deliverable:** A short presentation with a summary, impact, watchpoints, and sources.  
**Validation:** The changes match the official specification and announcement.

---

## Key takeaway

**Topic → verified sources → clear summary → impact → links → items to watch**

A technology watch informs and explains. It does not present a prediction as a fact.
